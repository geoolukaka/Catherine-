Mwea Pishori is a high-grade rice grown in Mwea, Kenya. Ours is 100% pure and sourced directly from Mwea, with no mixing.

How much does it cost?

The price is KSh 180 per kg. Use the calculator on this page to see your total for any quantity. Delivery is charged separately.

Yes. We supply both small and large quantities, from a few kilograms for your home to bulk orders for businesses.

How do I place an order?

Message Catherine on WhatsApp (+254 794 372 117) with your quantity and location. We confirm your total and delivery charge, and you pay before delivery.

No. Every order is 100% pure Mwea Pishori, with nothing mixed in.
